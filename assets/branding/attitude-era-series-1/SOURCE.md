# Attitude Era — Series 1 branding

The set mark is a local vector treatment of the 1997–2002 WWF scratch-era visual language: distressed white/silver angular WWF strokes with the red underline, adapted to WWE Legacy's black/charcoal/red Attitude Era palette.

Historical reference: WWE corporate materials describe the stylized WWF mark as the “scratch logo”; WWE changed its name and modified the scratch mark in May 2002.
