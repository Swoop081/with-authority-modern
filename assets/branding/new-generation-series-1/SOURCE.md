# New Generation — Series 1 branding source

## Canonical logo
`new-generation-logo.svg` is the WWF New Generation on-screen logo supplied for WWE Legacy Card Studio use.

- Source: Wikimedia Commons — `File:WWF New Gen Logo.svg`
- Original source/author identified by Commons: World Wrestling Federation
- Commons notes that the U.S. Copyright Office Review Board found copyright does not subsist in this logo and marks the file public domain as a simple text/geometric logo; trademark restrictions may still apply.
- Retrieved for this development build on 20 August 2026.

WWE Legacy does not recolour the logo itself. The surrounding Card Studio template uses a separate retro-1990s palette: indigo/violet, cyan, hot magenta and warm yellow.
