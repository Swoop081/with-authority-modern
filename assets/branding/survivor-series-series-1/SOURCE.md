# Survivor Series — Series 1 official branding

WWE Legacy uses the official **Survivor Series: WarGames 2026 — Houston** logo treatment published by WWE for the Nov. 28, 2026 event at Daikin Park.

Source page: https://www.wwe.com/shows/survivor-series-wargames/2026
Official WWE promotional image: https://www.wwe.com/f/styles/wwe_16_9_xl/public/2026/08/20260710_SurvivorSeries_Announcement_16x9.jpg

`survivor-series-wargames-houston-2026.png` is the transparent logo treatment used by the Card Art Studio and live presentation. The surrounding Legacy template derives its navy / orange / steel palette from the official 2026 Houston creative rather than the retired development-only red/blue placeholder identity.
