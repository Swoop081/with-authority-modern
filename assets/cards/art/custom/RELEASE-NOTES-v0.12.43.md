# WWE Legacy v0.12.43 — Roster Balance Pass

v0.12.43 supersedes v0.12.42 as the working baseline.

## What changed

This is the first complete roster rebalance performed after the core match flow was settled. It **does not change** retained-Control drawing, the actual-HP pin curve, connected-Move Adrenaline, Momentum placement, Entrance timing, counter terminality, or the persistent body-part submission engine.

The pass uses modest HP corrections where possible and identity-specific tuning where the v0.12.42 submission cleanup materially changed a wrestler's game plan.

### Key identity corrections

- **Mankind:** HP 68. Deranged Resilience now triggers the first 3 times he takes 7+ Move damage and reduces it by 3. Mr. Socko now correctly grants **+2 actual Mandible Claw submission damage** during that Control sequence instead of behaving like a cost discount.
- **CM Punk:** HP 63. Pipe Bomb triggers on the first 3 successful Counters. Anaconda Vise draws 1 page on connect.
- **Paige:** HP 64. Anti-Diva can trigger 3 times; PTO is P5.
- **Charlotte Flair:** HP 65. Figure-Eight Leglock is P6; Genetically Superior remains capped at one trigger.
- **Ultimate Warrior:** HP 67. Feel the Power can trigger twice and grants draw 1 + 1 Adrenaline.
- **Rhea Ripley:** HP 63; Prism Trap P5.
- **Brock Lesnar:** HP 67; Kimura Lock P6.
- **Logan Paul:** HP 61; Viral Athlete restores one Strike→Agility draw; Knockout Punch C9/D8; Paulverizer C11/D13.
- **Chad Gable:** HP 61; Olympic Pedigree sequence draw can trigger twice; Ankle Lock P6.
- **Danhausen:** HP 66; You Are Cursed! first 3 Control losses; Very Nice, Very Knee-vil searches Triple D and discounts it by 3 this Control sequence.
- **Gunther:** HP 65; Gojira Clutch P6.
- **Bron Breakker:** HP 65; Steiner Recliner P6.
- **André the Giant:** HP 66, placing him back inside the explicit 55–60% target while preserving Giant's Reach and C11/D18 Sitdown Splash.
- **The Rock / Goldberg:** HP 70 / 68 and remain the deliberate prestige tier.
