# WWE Legacy: Collectible Card Game — v0.11.65
## Money in the Bank Series 1 — LA Knight

- Adds fully playable **LA Knight** to Money in the Bank — Series 1.
- LA Knight: HP 52; Strike Unlimited / Strength 4 / Technical 3 / Agility 2; Momentum Strike x5 / Strength x3 / Technical x2 / Agility x2.
- Entrance **Let Me Talk to Ya!**: begin with +1 Strike Momentum and +1 Adrenaline.
- Ability **The Megastar**: first two connected Moves with 8+ printed Damage gain +1 Adrenaline; if LA Knight had 4+ Adrenaline before the Move connected, also draw 1 page.
- Superstar Special **YEAH!**: after Diving Elbow Drop connects, search/draw BFT and discount that searched BFT by 2 for the current Control sequence.
- Adds shared **Jumping Neckbreaker** (C5 / D8 / Technical 1 + Agility 1): grounds opponent; if played immediately after a Strike Move in the same Control sequence, draw 1 page.
- Adds shared **Burning Hammer** (C7 / D11 / Strength 2 + Technical 1): grounds opponent; Pin Bonus +2.
- Adds LA Knight-exclusive Finisher **BFT** (C9 / D16 / no Method requirement): grounds opponent; Pin Bonus +4, increased to +5 immediately after Diving Elbow Drop.
- Permanent collector IDs: `MITB1-006` Jumping Neckbreaker, `MITB1-007` Burning Hammer, `MITB1-008` BFT, `MITB1-009` Let Me Talk to Ya!, `MITB1-010` YEAH!, `MITB1-011` LA Knight.
- LA Knight recommended deck is exactly 55 pages / 12 Momentum.
- Money in the Bank Card Art Studio and Tonight’s Main Event branding remain enabled.
