# WWE Legacy v0.12.39 — Season Home Consolidation

## Home hub

The Home screen no longer presents two separate Season buttons that both open the same Season 1 hub.

- **Season One Live** and **Unlock the Final Boss** are consolidated into one premium Season 1 overview hero.
- The live Season countdown remains prominent.
- The hero now shows the current tier transition (for example, `TIER 12 → TIER 13`).
- It shows exact XP progress inside the current tier and the XP still required to reach the next tier.
- A bright green luminous progress bar visualizes progress to the next tier.
- The Rock / Final Boss remains visible as the Tier 50 destination inside the same hero rather than occupying a second Home tile.
- Survivor Series / Season end context remains visible.
- The consolidation frees vertical Home-screen space and removes duplicated navigation.

## Scope

Presentation/navigation only. No gameplay, balance, cards, decks, pin rules, Counter rules, AI, Momentum, Entrance, retained-Control draw, Adrenaline, collection, or Season economy values changed.
