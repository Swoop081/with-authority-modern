# WWE Legacy CCG v0.11.80 — Season 1 Balance Pass 1

First targeted full-roster balance pass after the 41-Superstar Season 1 roster and common wrestling expansion were completed. The pass uses a 3,280-match round-robin AI benchmark as a directional tool, while preserving The Rock as the deliberate Season 1 prestige outlier.

## Top-end trims
- Raquel Rodriguez — Unmatched Power now grants +1 Damage only on the first qualifying 8+ printed-Damage Strength Move. Tejana Bomb is C11 / D15 / Pin Bonus +4. Judgment Day Backup now reduces a qualifying hit by 1 rather than 3.
- Ultimate Warrior — Feel the Power now draws once per match rather than twice.
- Logan Paul — Viral Athlete keeps the first-Strike Strength unlock but only gets one Strike-to-Agility draw. The Maverick now begins with +1 Agility Momentum (Strength is earned through Viral Athlete) and Brass Knuckles is +2 Damage instead of +3.
- Kane — Big Red Machine now triggers once and grants +1 Adrenaline without the extra draw; Hellfire and Brimstone begins with +1 Technical Momentum and +1 Adrenaline.

## Significant catch-up buffs
- El Grande Americano — Masked Opportunist can trigger 3 times; Los Americanos begins with +1 in all four Methods and +1 Adrenaline; Steel Plate discount increased to 3.
- Charlotte Flair — Genetically Superior can trigger 3 times, draws 1 and discounts the next Agility Move by 2; All Hail the Queen begins with Technical + Agility + Strength Momentum; Natural Selection C6; Figure-Eight Leglock C8.
- LA Knight — The Megastar now triggers on 7+ printed Damage and always draws 1 alongside +1 Adrenaline; Let Me Talk to Ya! begins with Strike + Strength + Technical Momentum; BFT tutor discount remains 2.
- Sol Ruca — Good Vibes begins with Agility + Technical Momentum; Avalanche X-Factor C6; Sol Snatcher C9; Daredevil Instincts can trigger 3 times.

## Moderate buffs
- Brock Lesnar — Here Comes the Pain begins with Strength + Technical Momentum; Suplex City gains +2 Adrenaline on its German Suplex trigger; No Sell threshold reduced to 8 Damage.
- CM Punk — Pipe Bomb now triggers on the first 2 successful Counters, gaining +1 Technical Momentum and drawing 1 each time; It’s Clobbering Time also begins with +1 Strike Momentum.
- Kevin Owens — Prize Fighter now triggers at 6+ Damage; Fight Owens Fight also begins with +1 Strike Momentum.
- Rey Mysterio — Ultimate Underdog draws 2 on qualifying kickouts; Booyaka 619 also begins with +1 Technical Momentum; Lucha Libre Legend counter-attack bonus is +3 Damage.
- Penta — Cero Miedo begins with Agility + Strength + Technical Momentum; Zero Fear, Zero Mercy remains +2 Damage; Fearless Assault remains C2 discount / +2 Damage.
- Bayley — Role Model now also draws 1 on its first 3 Method switches.
- Stephanie Vaquer — La Primera now also draws 1; The Dark Angel begins with Technical + Agility Momentum.
- IYO SKY — Genius of the Sky now also draws 1; Tokyo Shock begins with Strength + Agility Momentum.
- Chelsea Green — Complaints Department still drains 1 Adrenaline once per Control sequence, with the first 2 successful Counter triggers also drawing 1.
- Dominik Mysterio — Dirty Dom begins with Agility + Strength + Technical Momentum while retaining his locked 49 HP.

## Round-robin result
The post-pass 3,280-match benchmark produced a much healthier regular-roster centre. Examples: Roman 61.9%, Charlotte 58.8%, Penta 58.8%, Logan 56.3%, Brock 55.6%, LA Knight 55.6%, IYO 49.4%, Owens 48.1%, Rey 47.5%, Chelsea 43.8%. The Rock remains intentionally clear at 81.3%.

Raquel remains a visible high-end outlier at 72.5%, while Tiffany Stratton and Becky Lynch are the clearest low-end AI-sim watches. Those are retained for the next playtest/simulation pass rather than over-correcting locked recent designs from one heuristic benchmark.
