# WWE Legacy v0.12.37 — Move Card Front Legibility Pass

This release is a Card Art Studio/export presentation pass only. Gameplay, AI, pin rules, Counter rules, cards, decks and Superstar balance remain unchanged from v0.12.36.

## Move front changes

- Removed the separate bordered Cost and Damage stat boxes from Move-card fronts.
- Cost and Damage now use large, heavyweight unboxed numerals positioned symmetrically at the lower left and lower right of the identity plate.
- The figures are vertically centered in the space between the Move name and the bottom of the card plate so they read as part of one clean lower information band.
- Cost/Damage labels remain small but are enlarged slightly and centered above each number.
- Method requirement text (for example `◆ 3 STRENGTH`) is substantially larger and heavier.
- `MOVE • <TYPE>` is substantially larger and heavier.
- Move name is shifted slightly upward to provide clearer separation between the name and the enlarged lower stats.
- The changes are rendered directly into exported Card Art Studio fronts, not just applied to the browser preview UI.

## Compatibility

- Build/cache stamps updated to v0.12.37.
- No gameplay data changes.
