# WWE Legacy v0.13.93 — Season One Cena + Attitude Rock

Content and presentation continuation of the verified v0.13.90 baseline. The abandoned v0.13.91 asset-path cleanup is not included.

## Era-logo corrections
- **Golden Era** card fronts and Card Art Studio use the clean classic WWF block logo only. The incorrect added `GOLDEN ERA` wording/underline treatment is not used.
- **Attitude Era** card fronts and Card Art Studio use the tightly cropped WWF scratch logo asset. In Card Art Studio the logo remains at the doubled v0.13.90 size and is re-centred in the upper-right card-corner zone (`82% / 10%`).

## Attitude Era — The People’s Champion
- **Chyna is parked** as internal future content and is no longer part of the player-live Attitude Era Series 1 roster.
- **The Rock — The People’s Champion** takes the eighth live Attitude Era slot.
- New Rock package: The Rock’s Spinebuster, The Rock’s Samoan Drop, Rock Bottom, People’s Elbow, **If You Smell…** Entrance, and **Know Your Role** Action.
- Know Your Role is a true one-use Superstar Action that searches Rock Bottom or People’s Elbow and gives the selected move a 1-cost discount for the current Control sequence.
- Rock’s authored 60-page deck carries 12 Momentum and a 10-page Counter density, within the established 7–10 target range.

## Season 1 — The Last Time Is Now
- The previous **Final Boss Rock** Season 1 package is parked internally as a future reward and is no longer the active Season Road chase.
- **John Cena — The Last Time Is Now** is the new babyface Season 1 chase/reward Superstar.
- Cena package: Protobomb, Five Knuckle Shuffle, STF, Attitude Adjustment, Hustle Loyalty Respect, **The Time Is Now** Entrance, and **Never Give Up** Action.
- Season 1 is rebuilt as a **30-day / 50-tier** chase beginning 22 August 2026 and ending 21 September 2026.
- Each tier remains **100 XP**, making the complete Season chase **5,000 XP**.
- The road awards **five separate Normal copies of every Cena-exclusive Move**: Protobomb, Five Knuckle Shuffle, STF and Attitude Adjustment.
- Cena’s **Hustle, Loyalty, Respect** support and **Never Give Up** Action are also earned on the road.
- Tier 48 awards the Ruby **The Time Is Now** Entrance and Tier 50 awards the Ruby **John Cena — The Last Time Is Now** Superstar identity.
- Non-Cena tiers continue to interleave released-set boosters and UP; unreleased sets remain gated out of Season booster rewards.
- Final Boss Rock remains authored internally for a future reward release rather than being deleted.

## Artwork policy
- No replacement artwork is invented for the new Rock/Cena content. Missing custom art continues to use the canonical rules/details fallback until supplied artwork is installed.

## Unchanged systems
- Four-tier Normal / Emerald / Sapphire / Ruby collection rules remain unchanged.
- Fresh-start Punk/Roman + full Welcome Superstar onboarding remains unchanged.
- Reward economy remains unchanged.
- Live player set pool remains SummerSlam, Evolution, New Generation, Golden Era and Attitude Era. RAW remains banked.
