## Audit findings and changes
### Ultimate Warrior
Primary problem: Warrior Splash was being found reliably, but his deck was still slow to generate enough useful hand flow before the finish.
Change: Feel the Power keeps its original two +1 Attitude triggers; the first qualifying 6+ damage connection now also draws 1 page.
Result: 49.8% overall.

### Stone Cold Steve Austin
Primary problem: Stone Cold's Kick to the Gut found the Stunner often, but the searched Stunner sat dead at cost 10 and the Kick sequence did not replenish Austin's hand.
Change: Kick to the Gut now gives the searched Stunner a persistent -2 total-cost modifier, gains +1 Attitude, and draws 1 page.
Result: 48.8% overall.

### Liv Morgan
Primary problem: Technical Momentum was too scarce for Code Red/Gotcha while Strike was slightly overrepresented. Watch Me was also triggering too late.
Change: Momentum shifts from 5 Agility / 4 Strike / 3 Technical to 5 Agility / 3 Strike / 4 Technical; Watch Me triggers at 40% HP instead of 35%.
Result: 46.0% overall.

### Hulk Hogan
Primary problem: Hogan's Big Boot found Atomic Leg Drop, but Leg Drop was regularly stranded by total Momentum and Strength gates.
Change: Hogan's Big Boot now gives Atomic Leg Drop a persistent -2 cost modifier, +1 Strength, +1 Attitude, and draws 1.
Result: 45.5% overall.

### Brock Lesnar
Primary problem: Brock's German Suplex chain worked, but his opening/midgame hand could still run dry before Suplex City converted into the later power game.
Change: the first Brock's German Suplex to connect each match draws 1 page. The established 3-German chain and opponent draw-2 mechanic remain unchanged.
Result: 49.2% overall.
