## Lead-design conclusion

The later four-Superstar sets are now extremely even at 28–29 cards after the common-wrestling expansion, and there are no orphan gameplay cards. That is healthy structurally, but they remain intentionally much smaller than the launch sets. Future common Moves, Reversals, Submissions and Action cards should continue to be added only when they are authentic, reusable wrestling vocabulary and verified absent from the live pool. A sensible future target is roughly **32–40 cards per later set**, reached organically rather than by duplicating existing moves or inventing filler.

No additional pool cards were manufactured in v0.11.82; this pass audits and protects the v0.11.79 expansion baseline.
