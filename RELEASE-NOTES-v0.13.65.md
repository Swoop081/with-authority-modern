# WWE Legacy v0.13.65 — Shared Move Triple Pass

**Frozen:** 20 August 2026

v0.13.65 adds three shared booster moves across existing Series 1 collector sets. No authored Superstar deck is changed.

## New cards

### Vertical Boston Crab — EVO1-075
- **2★ Uncommon**
- **Cost 6 / Damage 0**
- **Technical 2**
- **Submission / Rear Control**
- Grounded opponent only.
- **+4 persistent Back damage per successful turn.**
- Shared / booster-only.

### Diving Shoulder Block — HOF1-094
- **1★ Common**
- **Cost 4 / Damage 6**
- **Agility 1**
- **Aerial / Diving Aerial**
- Grounds opponent.
- Shared / booster-only.
- This is a separate generic card from **Ultimate Warrior’s existing exclusive Diving Shoulder Block**; the Warrior card is not replaced or retuned.

### Springboard Roundhouse Kick — SD1-038
- **3★ Rare**
- **Cost 6 / Damage 9**
- **Agility 2 + Strike 1**
- **Aerial / Running Aerial**
- Grounds opponent.
- Shared / booster-only.

## Collector placement
- Evolution — Series 1 expands to **75 cards, EVO1-001–EVO1-075, gap-free**.
- Hall of Fame — Series 1 expands to **94 cards, HOF1-001–HOF1-094, gap-free**.
- SmackDown — Series 1 expands to **38 cards, SD1-001–SD1-038, gap-free**.
- SmackDown retains its existing future-set release gating; this pass does not release it early.

## Card Studio
All three cards are available in Card Studio under their assigned sets and inherit those sets’ existing Studio presentation.

## Scope
All v0.13.64 and earlier accepted gameplay, UI, economy, progression, release gating and collector systems remain authoritative.
