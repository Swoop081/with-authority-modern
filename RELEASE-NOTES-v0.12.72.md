- Renames **Electric Chair Facebuster** to **Electric Chair Drop** everywhere while preserving the existing card ID and gameplay.
- Restores the Season Daily Login Booster to one full-width purple state control: **CLAIM FREE BOOSTER** when ready and **NEXT FREE BOOSTER IN …** during cooldown.

- Special Ladder/Championship reward-pack wrappers now use the short face title **REWARD**; detailed reward type remains below the pack tile.
