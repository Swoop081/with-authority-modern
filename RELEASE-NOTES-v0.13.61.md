# WWE Legacy v0.13.61 — New Generation Diesel Big Daddy Cool Pass

## New Generation — Series 1
Diesel completes the foundational four-Superstar 1993–1995 New Generation roster alongside Bret Hart, Shawn Michaels and Razor Ramon. New Generation remains an undated, development-only future set and is not exposed on player-facing release surfaces.

### Diesel — NG1-028
- **Nickname:** Big Daddy Cool
- **HP:** 69
- **Method limits:** Strength Unlimited / Strike 4 / Technical 2 / Agility 1
- **Starter Momentum:** 7 Strength / 4 Strike / 1 Technical
- **Ability — Big Daddy Cool:** Once per match, after Diesel takes 8+ Damage from a connected Move, gain +1 Adrenaline.
- Authored 60-page heavyweight Strength/Strike baseline deck. As with all recommended decks, it is a thematic starting point rather than a claim of meta-optimal construction.

### Diesel-exclusive cards
- **NG1-022 — Diesel’s Snake Eyes** — 3★ Rare Trademark; C5/D8; Strength 1; Grapple / Body Elevated; grounds opponent; on Connect the next Diesel’s Big Boot costs 1 less this Control sequence.
- **NG1-023 — Diesel’s Big Boot** — 3★ Rare Trademark; C5/D8; Strike 2; Strike / Leg Extended; grounds opponent; on Connect the next Diesel’s Sidewalk Slam costs 1 less this Control sequence.
- **NG1-024 — Diesel’s Sidewalk Slam** — 3★ Rare Trademark; C6/D10; Strength 2; Grapple / Torso Trapped; grounds opponent; on Connect search/draw Jackknife Powerbomb and it costs 1 less this Control sequence.
- **NG1-025 — Jackknife Powerbomb** — 4★ Very Rare Finisher; C11/D18; no Method requirement; Grapple / Body Elevated; grounds opponent. The C11 elite Cost premium follows the locked >17 Damage rule.
- **NG1-026 — Diesel Power** — 4★ Very Rare Entrance; +1 Strength Momentum, +1 Strike Momentum and +1 Adrenaline through normal Entrance timing.
- **NG1-027 — Two Dudes with Attitudes** — 4★ Very Rare Special Action; once per match during Control, search/draw one Diesel-exclusive Trademark and give it -1 Cost this Control sequence.
