# WWE Legacy: Collectible Card Game

Current working build: **v0.13.32 — Future Shared Move Expansion Pass**.

A mobile-first WWE collectible card game prototype with 60-page Superstar decks, Method Momentum, Adrenaline, Counters, Submissions, Pins, Deck Lab, Collection, boosters, Store, Seasons, Challenges, Live Events, Money in the Bank, King of the Ring and Championship Road.

## Current build
- Adds eight approved shared future-set cards across RAW Series 1, Worlds Collide Series 1, SmackDown Series 1 and Survivor Series Series 1.
- RAW: **Leg Lariat** and **Double Foot Stomp**.
- Worlds Collide: **Stomp to the Arm** and **Top Rope Splash**.
- SmackDown: **Kick to the Back** and **Reverse Chin Lock**.
- Survivor Series: **Death Valley Driver** and **Leg Kick**.
- All eight are booster-only and remain hidden until their assigned set is released.
- Automatic Layered v1 artwork priority/fallback from v0.13.31 remains active.
- Kevin Owens’ v0.13.30 Rare Trademarks and MITB shared move package remain locked.
- Superstar chase remains **2% natural with one global 100-miss pity**.
- Secondary Superstar unlocks remain lean: Superstar + at most 1 Finisher / 1 Trademark / 1 Action.
- Expanded Achievements / AP / Career Score remains on hold.
- Profile schema remains **30**.

Season 1 release schedule remains: **RAW — Series 1 (5 Sep 2026)**, **Worlds Collide — Series 1 (26 Sep 2026)**, **Money in the Bank — Series 1 (10 Oct 2026)**, **SmackDown — Series 1 (31 Oct 2026)** and **Survivor Series — Series 1 (28 Nov 2026)**.
