# WWE Legacy v0.13.93 — Welcome iPhone Presentation Hotfix

This build supersedes v0.13.92. It is a presentation-only onboarding hotfix: gameplay, balance, Season 1 Cena, Attitude Era Rock, the four-tier system, rewards, live-set availability and fresh-start grants are unchanged.

## Welcome onboarding fixes

- The five Welcome era choices use their live set branding directly; no fake booster-pack artwork is introduced because the reward is a full random Superstar, not a booster.
- The era choice layout is compacted for iPhone so the fifth SummerSlam choice no longer sits far below oversized empty tiles.
- The awarded Welcome Superstar now renders as a true full-size collectible-card hero instead of collapsing to a thin horizontal strip.
- The reveal fills the iPhone viewport and anchors the Continue CTA at the bottom, eliminating the large unused black area.

Current verified-development candidate built from **v0.13.90 — Era Logo Presentation Hotfix**. The abandoned v0.13.91 asset-path consolidation work is intentionally excluded.

## Current live content direction
Five player-facing sets remain live: **SummerSlam — Series 1, Evolution — Series 1, New Generation — Series 1, Golden Era — Series 1, and Attitude Era — Series 1**. RAW and other banked future sets remain unavailable until a later build explicitly releases them.

Attitude Era Series 1 now uses **The Rock — The People’s Champion** in the eighth Superstar slot. **Chyna is parked** as internal future content. The former Season 1 **Final Boss Rock** package is also banked for a future reward rather than deleted.

## Season 1
Season 1 is now a **30-day, 50-tier** babyface **John Cena — The Last Time Is Now** chase. Each tier remains 100 XP, for **5,000 XP total**. Across the road the player earns **five Normal copies each** of Protobomb, Five Knuckle Shuffle, STF and Attitude Adjustment, plus Cena’s support and Action. Tier 48 awards the **Ruby The Time Is Now Entrance** and Tier 50 awards the **Ruby John Cena** Superstar identity. Non-Cena tiers continue to mix released-set boosters and UP.

## Fresh-start onboarding
New saves choose **CM Punk or Roman Reigns** and receive a complete 60-page **Normal-tier** starter deck. The one-time Welcome choice then offers SummerSlam, Evolution, New Generation, Golden Era or Attitude Era and grants one random eligible Superstar from the chosen set plus that Superstar’s complete 60-page Normal deck. A SummerSlam Welcome draw excludes the already selected starter.

## Four-tier collection
Collectible printings remain **Normal / Emerald / Sapphire / Ruby**. Sapphire is the authored balance baseline; eligible Move Damage is Normal −2, Emerald −1, Sapphire authored/current, Ruby +1. Submission pressure follows the same ladder. Each card identity holds up to five copies independently in each tier; copy 6+ of that tier converts to UP. CPU opponents mirror the player’s tier strength by comparable role/class.

## Reward economy
- Match win: **5 XP**; loss: **0 XP**.
- Exhibition: **1 random released-set booster every 5 wins**.
- Daily Challenge: **10 XP**.
- Weekly Challenge: **25 XP + 1 random booster**.
- Money in the Bank: **2 random boosters for clearing all 8 matches**.
- Live Event: **1 random booster for clearing all 5 matches**.
- King of the Ring: **1 random booster for winning the tournament**.
- Championship Road: **1 themed booster every completed four-match block**.
- Free Season booster: **1 every 24 hours**.
- Collection/Ruby milestones: **1 random booster at 25/50/75/100%**.
- Super Packs remain retired.

## Era branding
Golden Era uses the clean classic WWF block logo without added text. Attitude Era uses the cropped WWF scratch logo, enlarged and re-centred in the card’s top-right branding zone.
