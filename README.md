# WWE Legacy: Collectible Card Game

Current working build: **v0.13.33 — Legend Trademark + Shared Move Pass**.

A mobile-first WWE collectible card game prototype with 60-page Superstar decks, Method Momentum, Adrenaline, Counters, Submissions, Pins, Deck Lab, Collection, boosters, Store, Seasons, Challenges, Live Events, Money in the Bank, King of the Ring and Championship Road.

## Current build
- Removes the duplicate **RAW1-041 Double Foot Stomp**; RAW Series 1 now ends at **RAW1-040 Leg Lariat**.
- Renames the existing **EVO1-034 Double Stomp** to **Double Foot Stomp** without changing its collector identity or gameplay.
- Adds shared **Wheelbarrow Suplex — SVS1-053** and **Test of Strength — HOF1-092**.
- Adds André’s Rare Trademarks **André’s Choke — HOF1-090** and **André’s Bear Hug — HOF1-091**, with two copies of each installed in André’s authored 60-page deck.
- Adds Randy Savage’s Rare Trademark **Macho’s Double Axe Handle — HOF1-093** at three copies in his authored 60-page deck.
- The generic **Double Axe Handle** keeps its C4/D6 shared profile but no longer carries Savage-specific text.
- All v0.13.32 future shared additions not explicitly superseded here remain locked.
- Automatic Layered v1 artwork priority/fallback from v0.13.31 remains active.
- Superstar chase remains **2% natural with one global 100-miss pity**.
- Secondary Superstar unlocks remain lean: Superstar + at most 1 Finisher / 1 Trademark / 1 Action.
- Expanded Achievements / AP / Career Score remains on hold.
- Profile schema remains **30**.

Season 1 release schedule remains: **RAW — Series 1 (5 Sep 2026)**, **Worlds Collide — Series 1 (26 Sep 2026)**, **Money in the Bank — Series 1 (10 Oct 2026)**, **SmackDown — Series 1 (31 Oct 2026)** and **Survivor Series — Series 1 (28 Nov 2026)**.
