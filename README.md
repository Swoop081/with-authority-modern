# WWE Legacy: Collectible Card Game — v0.13.89

Current verified development build: **Welcome Full Superstar Start**.

## Fresh-start onboarding
New players begin by choosing **CM Punk or Roman Reigns**. Every card in that starter package is a **Normal** printing.

Immediately afterward, the player receives a one-time **Welcome Superstar** choice between **Evolution, New Generation, Golden Era, Attitude Era, or SummerSlam**. The game randomly selects one eligible Superstar from the chosen set and grants that Superstar plus a complete, playable **60-page Normal deck**. If SummerSlam is chosen, the starter Superstar already selected is removed from the draw, leaving a seven-Superstar random pool.

The result is a fresh profile with **two complete playable Superstars** from the start, while Emerald, Sapphire and Ruby remain pack/progression upgrades.

## Fresh Normal deck legality
Every granted fresh-start deck respects the universal five-copy-per-tier ownership rule. Older authored blueprints that used more than five copies of one Momentum colour are normalized only by redistributing the excess Momentum pages; the deck remains 60 pages and all Moves/Actions remain unchanged.

## Four-tier collection
Every collectible card uses **Normal / Emerald / Sapphire / Ruby** printings. Sapphire is the authored/current balance baseline. For Moves, Normal is −2 Damage, Emerald −1, Sapphire unchanged, and Ruby +1. Submission pressure follows the same −2/−1/0/+1 ladder with its existing minimum floor. Cost and authored secondary effects remain unchanged. Intrinsic card rarity never changes between tiers.

Ownership is independent by printing: **5 Normal + 5 Emerald + 5 Sapphire + 5 Ruby** may be kept for the same underlying card. Copy 6+ of a specific tier overflows to Universe Points. CPU opponents keep their own authored decks but mirror the player's tier strength by comparable role/class.

## Live collection
Five player-facing sets are live: **SummerSlam — Series 1, Evolution — Series 1, New Generation — Series 1, Golden Era — Series 1, and Attitude Era — Series 1**. RAW and other authored future sets remain banked until a later build explicitly releases them. There is no automatic release calendar or public roadmap.

## Reward economy
The milestone-focused economy remains active: **5 XP per win, 0 XP on losses**; Exhibition awards 1 random booster every 5 wins; Daily Challenge awards 10 XP; Weekly Challenge awards 25 XP + 1 random booster; Money in the Bank awards 2 random boosters for an eight-match clear; Live Events award 1 random booster for a five-match clear; King of the Ring awards 1 random booster for winning; Championship Road awards 1 themed booster every four matches; and the free 24-hour booster remains. Season Road rewards remain unchanged.
