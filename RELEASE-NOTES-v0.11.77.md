# WWE Legacy: Collectible Card Game — v0.11.77
## SmackDown Series 1 — Tiffany Stratton + Chelsea Green

- Adds **Tiffany Stratton**, SmackDown Series 1 Superstar #2 after Danhausen.
- Tiffany: 50 HP; Agility Unlimited / Strength 4 / Technical 1 / Strike 1; starter Momentum Agility 7 / Strength 4 / Technical 1.
- Adds **It’s Tiffy Time**, **Tiffy Time**, **Tiffany Epiphany**, **Handspring Back Elbow**, and **Prettiest Moonsault Ever**.
- Tiffany uses Strength setup into discounted Agility offence, culminating in PME.
- Adds shared SmackDown moves **Front Kick**, **Snap Suplex**, and **Finlay Roll**.
- Adds **Chelsea Green** with a counter-control/opportunist identity.
- Chelsea: 49 HP; Technical Unlimited / Agility 3 / Strike 2 / Strength 1; starter Momentum Technical 7 / Agility 3 / Strike 2.
- Adds **Hot Mess**, **The Complaints Department**, **File a Complaint**, **I’m Prettier**, and **Green With Envy**.
- Chelsea's recommended starter deliberately carries a heavier Counter package than the average deck.
- Both recommended starters are 55 pages with 12 Momentum pages and respect the five-copy cap.
- SmackDown collector numbering now extends through SD1-022.
- Both Superstars are development-unlocked for immediate playtest and Card Art Studio work.
