# WWE Legacy v0.11.43 — Momentum Card Mockups

## Momentum fronts
- Added built-in premium mockup fronts for the four active shared Momentum cards:
  - SS1-064 Agility Momentum
  - SS1-065 Strength Momentum
  - SS1-066 Strike Momentum
  - SS1-067 Technical Momentum
- All four share one visual family while using distinct method colour, emblem and two-letter identity.
- Fronts emphasize `+1`, method name and `PERMANENT MOMENTUM`; gameplay rules and rarity remain on the back.
- Existing custom Card Art Studio WebPs still take priority automatically when installed, so the mockups are safe defaults rather than a locked art direction.

## Card Art Studio
- Momentum cards now preview with their built-in mockup without requiring a source image.
- The built-in Momentum front can be exported directly from the Studio.
- Loading replacement artwork still switches back to the normal artwork + front-identity workflow.

## Compatibility
- No cards were added, removed or renumbered.
- No deck, gameplay, balance, collection or rarity changes.
- Cache-busting stamped to v0.11.43.
