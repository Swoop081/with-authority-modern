# WWE Legacy v0.12.18 — Expanded Reversal Matrix

## Counter accessibility
- Most fundamental reversal Moves no longer carry a Method Momentum requirement.
- Of the original eight state-anchor counters, only Dropkick and Hurricanrana retain Agility 1.
- Knees Up, Punch, Drop Toe Hold, Headbutt, Arm Drag and Back Elbow are now usable without a printed Method requirement.
- Other simple fundamentals including Duck, Sidestep, Elbow, Reverse Elbow, Knee to the Gut and Hip Toss are also ungated by Method Momentum.

## With Authority-inspired reversal pool
Nine generic reversal techniques were added from the legacy WA move vocabulary: Dodge, Block, Up and Over, Standing Switch, Rollover Counter, Backflip Counter, Catch the Foot, Arm Drag Counter and Jawbreaker.

## Eight-state reversal coverage
Every one of the eight physical attack states now has at least four distinct reversal cards available in the global card pool.

## Submission response coverage
Every Submission target — Arm, Leg, Back and Neck / Head — now has at least four linked response cards. Jawbreaker specifically answers Neck / Head submissions; Catch the Foot answers Leg submissions; Arm Drag Counter answers Arm submissions; Standing Switch and Rollover Counter expand Back-defense coverage.

## Recommended decks
- All 50 recommended decks remain exactly 60 pages with exactly 12 Momentum.
- Every recommended deck now carries at least 9 counter-capable pages.
- Every recommended deck has at least one response route to all eight physical counter states and all four Submission body areas.
- The original 55-page identity core is preserved wherever possible; v0.12.17's five extension slots are rebuilt around the new reversal package, with only low-value duplicate generic Moves replaced when more counter density is needed.
- Untouched v0.12.17 recommended 60-page saved decks migrate to the new package; customized decks are not overwritten.
