# WWE Legacy v0.11.97 — Survivor Series Series 1 Roster Expansion

Built on v0.11.96. Adds the four newly locked Survivor Series Series 1 Superstars: Drew McIntyre, Randy Orton, Sami Zayn and Jacob Fatu. Bron Breakker remains the existing first Superstar in the set.

## Drew McIntyre
- HP 55; Strength Unlimited / Strike 5 / Technical 2 / Agility 1.
- Scottish Warrior entrance; Pick Your Shot ability; Claymore Countdown special.
- Glasgow Kiss, Future Shock DDT (Trademark), Claymore (Finisher).

## Randy Orton
- HP 53; Technical Unlimited / Strength 4 / Strike 4 / Agility 2.
- Voices entrance; Apex Predator ability; Outta Nowhere special.
- Draping DDT (Trademark), RKO (Finisher), Punt Kick (second Finisher).
- Outta Nowhere lets RKO function as a once-per-match Counter at -2 cost.

## Sami Zayn
- HP 51; Technical Unlimited / Agility 5 / Strike 3 / Strength 2.
- Worlds Apart entrance; Underdog From the Underground ability; Never Say Die special.
- Exploder Suplex Into Turnbuckle, Blue Thunder Bomb (Trademark), Helluva Kick (Finisher).

## Jacob Fatu
- HP 56; Strength Unlimited / Strike 5 / Agility 4 / Technical 1.
- Samoan Werewolf entrance; All Gas, No Brakes ability; Built Different special.
- Pop-Up Samoan Drop (Trademark), Moonsault (Finisher), Tongan Death Grip (Submission Finisher).
