## Audit findings
### Randy Savage
Flying Elbow was searched reliably, but often sat at the cost-9 gate. Diving Double Axe Handle now gives the searched Flying Elbow a persistent -2 total-cost modifier and draws 1, while preserving its existing +2 Attitude.
Result: 45.6% overall.

### Stephanie Vaquer
Her main issue was not SVB damage; it was hand flow while changing methods. La Primera still gives +1 Attitude on the first two method changes, and the first such change now also draws 1.
Result: 50.8% overall.

### IYO SKY
IYO German Suplex was the largest repeated dead-card source because the deck only carries two Technical Momentum. The two IYO German copies were replaced with Hurricanrana rather than adding more Technical Momentum to an aerial deck.
Result: 49.0% overall.

### Hulk Hogan
Focused testing put Hogan around 48.8%, and further buffs risked overshooting because Big Boot -> Atomic Leg Drop is now functioning. No additional change was made in this pass.
Result: 44.6% in this particular full-field matrix; continue monitoring.

### Rhea Ripley
Prism Trap's Technical gate was the clearest repeated bottleneck. One Strike Momentum page moved to Technical, producing 5 Strength / 3 Strike / 4 Technical.
Result: 46.8% overall.
