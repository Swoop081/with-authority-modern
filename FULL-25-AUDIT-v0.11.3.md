## Audit findings
The 16-Superstar SummerSlam/Hall baseline remains mechanically sound. The approved Evolution designs are reasonably differentiated internally, but the set is materially weaker in cross-set play. This is structural, not an engine-stall problem.

Rhea Ripley: expensive Riptide/Prism Trap/Avalanche Riptide path frequently sits below total-Momentum thresholds.
Liv Morgan: Code Red finds Oblivion reliably, but Standing posture and total-Momentum gates strand Oblivion too often.
Becky Lynch: currently the strongest Evolution crossover performer; her Manhandle Slam -> Dis-Arm-Her path works more reliably than most Evolution endgames.
Bayley: internally strong, but Agility-heavy mid/high cards are frequently stranded while her Technical/Strike core still performs.
Charlotte Flair: the largest Evolution problem. Figure Eight is heavily gated by 3 Technical + 1 Agility and 10 total Momentum; The Queen rarely completes all three methods before the match gets away from her.
IYO SKY: Moonstomp finds Over the Moonsault consistently, but 3 Agility and the cost-10 gate remain difficult against the stronger sets.
Paige: Ram-Paige finds PTO, but PTO's Agility requirement and cost-9 gate strand the searched finisher too often.
Stephanie Vaquer: Package Backbreaker finds SVB, but the deliberately scarce Agility method is the dominant bottleneck.
Final Boss Rock: his resource-control identity is functioning and Bloodline Rules now applies its real +1 Attitude Counter tax, but his high-end Rock Bottom/Spinebuster -> People's Elbow route develops too slowly for the current 24-Superstar environment.

## Recommended next balance pass
Do not use blanket HP increases. First fix the structural bridges:
1. Rhea — Riptide should supply part of the resource bridge into the chosen finisher.
2. Charlotte — Figure Four should actively reduce Figure Eight's effective cost as originally designed.
3. IYO — Moonstomp's searched Over the Moonsault discount needs to be stronger/reliably preserved.
4. Paige — successful Counter/Ram-Paige should help establish the PTO Agility gate.
5. Stephanie — Package Backbreaker should establish or temporarily satisfy SVB's Agility requirement.
6. Final Boss Rock — his searched People's Elbow route needs faster Attitude/Strike access; preserve the locked 7/8/10 signature costs.
7. Liv — clean up Code Red -> Oblivion posture/discount behavior before touching damage.
8. Bayley — reduce dead Agility filler rather than buffing Rose Plant.
