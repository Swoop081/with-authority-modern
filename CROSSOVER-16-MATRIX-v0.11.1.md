## Main findings
- The crossover is healthy at engine level: zero stalls over 5,120 matches.
- SummerSlam is materially stronger as a set in direct crossover play.
- André remains strong, but his 54.7% record against SummerSlam is much less alarming than his Hall-only dominance.

- Kane is weak against both sets, indicating a deck/engine identity problem rather than only a set-power mismatch.
- Roman Reigns is also underperforming and should be reviewed on the SummerSlam side.
- Austin and Mankind are close to a useful cross-set centre point.
