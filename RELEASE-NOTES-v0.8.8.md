# WWE Legacy: Collectible Card Game — v0.8.8

## Season 1 Tier 50 — The Rock: The Final Boss
- Added a distinct modern Final Boss persona for The Rock. This identity is intentionally separate from future Attitude Era, Hollywood and other Rock versions.
- Tier 50 now awards The Rock plus his complete 55-page Season 1 deck as genuine collection ownership.
- The Final Boss Superstar and linked Entrance are Foil-only, consistent with the v0.8.7 collectible rule.
- The Season-exclusive set is not part of normal booster pools and is reserved for Season completion / future post-season Store availability.

## Final Boss identity
- 56 HP
- Ability: The first three times Rock connects a Strength or Strike Move for 5+ damage, draw 1 page and gain +1 Attitude.
- Attached Entrance: Final Boss Has Arrived — begin with +1 Strength; Turn 8 grants +1 Attitude.
- Lead Off five: Strength Momentum, Strike Momentum, Body Shot, Shoulder Block, Final Boss Clothesline.
- Signature package: Rock Bottom — Final Boss, People's Elbow — Final Boss, Final Boss Spinebuster, Final Boss Sharpshooter.
- Full deck is Strength/Strike-led with Technical support and Final Boss-themed utility.
