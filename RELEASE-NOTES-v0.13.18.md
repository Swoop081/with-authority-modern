# WWE Legacy v0.13.18 — Superstar Starter Deck + Recommended Build Assistance

## Secondary Superstar unlocks
- The player's very first Superstar remains the onboarding exception and receives the complete authored 60-page deck.
- Every later normal Superstar unlock now installs a legal owned 60-page **starter deck**, not the completed authored chase deck.
- The unlock grants one additional copy of each Superstar-exclusive identity card in that Superstar's recommended deck, seeds the shared Common/Uncommon identities needed for the starter, keeps one Once Too Often available, and fills the remaining 60-page structure with legal shared Common/Uncommon replacements.
- Superstar-specific Entrances are still not granted. Amazing Entrance remains equipped until the player's authored Entrance is collected.
- Older profiles never lose cards. Complete decks/ownership legitimately granted by older builds are preserved. Legacy identity-only Store unlocks with no playable deck are upgraded to the new starter package on migration.

## Deck Lab — Recommended Build
- Deck Lab now compares the current 60-page deck against the Superstar's authored recommended blueprint.
- A new Recommended Build panel shows exact matched slots, completion percentage, cards still to collect, owned upgrades that are ready now, and which current cards are serving as replacements.
- **Optimize Owned / Build Toward Recommended** construct the closest legal 60-page version possible from the player's Collection, preferring authored cards and filling unavailable slots with sensible legal replacements.
- Owned Foils remain preferred cosmetically with no gameplay bonus.

## Deck Assistance
- Pulling another copy of a Finisher, Trademark, Action or other card that belongs in an unlocked Superstar's recommended build can now replace starter filler through the existing safe Deck Assistance flow.
- Pulling the Superstar-specific Entrance creates an **Entrance Upgrade** recommendation over Amazing Entrance. Ask mode offers the change; Auto-upgrade equips it; Manual mode only surfaces the suggestion.
- Booster Superstar unlock packages are applied after all five booster cards are rolled so the starter grant cannot alter the remaining cards in the same pack.

## Rules / data
- Profile schema advances to 30.
- My Legacy Rulebook now explains first-Superstar full-deck onboarding versus later starter-deck/recommended-build progression.
- No card balance, HP, booster rarity odds, UP prices, duplicate values, release dates or artwork assets changed.
