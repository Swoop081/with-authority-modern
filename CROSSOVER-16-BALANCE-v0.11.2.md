# WWE Legacy v0.11.2 — 16-Superstar Crossover Balance Pass

## Diagnostic focus
Randy Savage, Hulk Hogan, The Undertaker, Kane, Roman Reigns, with Ultimate Warrior added after the crossover exposed the same bottleneck.

## Structural fixes
- Fixed Entrance scheduled triggers so ON_MOVE_CONNECTED and ON_DAMAGE_TAKEN effects actually execute.
- Randy Savage: Ooh Yeah now provides the intended card flow, Strength filler reduced, Diving Double Axe Handle better bridges into Flying Elbow.
- Hulk Hogan: Hulkamania now draws 2; Hulking Up can trigger at the meaningful 40% comeback threshold.
- Undertaker: Chokeslam now grants the Strength/Attitude bridge needed for Tombstone; Deadman Walking now creates an actual comeback draw.
- Kane: Through Hellfire discount is preserved for a meaningful high-cost Move; Chokeslam better bridges to Tombstone; Big Red Machine now draws 1 and gains +1 Attitude on its first two 8+ damage connections, with the approved next-Strike damage escalation.
- Roman Reigns: Corner Clotheslines -> Superman Punch -> Spear is now a reliable three-step chain.
- Ultimate Warrior: Gorilla Press Slam requirement changed from 3 Strength to 2 Strength + 1 Strike and now provides an Attitude bridge into Warrior Splash.
