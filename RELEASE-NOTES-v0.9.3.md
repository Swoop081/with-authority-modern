# WWE Legacy: Collectible Card Game — v0.9.3

## WWE.com Superstar portrait rollout
- Replaced the Hall of Fame, Evolution and Final Boss placeholder portraits with local WWE.com-style transparent Superstar profile portraits.
- Added local profile portrait assets for:
  Hulk Hogan, Andre the Giant, Randy Savage, Ultimate Warrior, Stone Cold Steve Austin, The Undertaker, Mankind/Mick Foley, Kane,
  Rhea Ripley, Liv Morgan, Becky Lynch, Bayley, Charlotte Flair, IYO SKY, Paige, Stephanie Vaquer and The Rock.
- SummerSlam Series 1 keeps its existing approved Superstar portrait set.
- All 25 playable Superstar cards now have dedicated local portrait art.

## Clean two-stage launch flow
- Removed the three booster-release promos from the initial splash.
- Screen 1 is now focused on:
  WWE Legacy branding,
  the actual Final Boss Rock Superstar card,
  COMPLETE SEASON 1 / UNLOCK THE FINAL BOSS,
  and the New Player / Start New Legacy (or returning-player) entry.
- First-time players still choose CM Punk or Roman Reigns in onboarding.
- After choosing a starter, players move to a dedicated Season 1 release-discovery screen.
- The release screen shows two actual Superstar cards for each set:
  - SummerSlam — Cody Rhodes + Brock Lesnar
  - Hall of Fame — Hulk Hogan + Stone Cold Steve Austin
  - Evolution — Rhea Ripley + Becky Lynch
- Roman Reigns and CM Punk are deliberately omitted from the SummerSlam showcase because one may already be the player's starter.
- Each set includes a TAKE ME THERE button that jumps directly to that Booster set.
- A Continue to WWE Legacy button proceeds to the Main Menu.

## Card rarity presentation
- Removed hollow filler stars.
- Cards now show exactly their rarity star count.
- Card stars are gold across card fronts and rules backs.
- Superstar card footer label now says STARS instead of RARITY, allowing the four-star row to fit cleanly.

## Home-screen icon
- Added WWE Legacy icon files at 180, 192 and 512 px.
- Added Apple touch-icon metadata and a web-app manifest.
- Add to Home Screen / pinned installs can now display the WWE Legacy game icon.

## Certification
