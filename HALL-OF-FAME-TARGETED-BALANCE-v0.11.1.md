## Changes
### André the Giant
- Bearhug: cost 8, damage 8, submission pressure 3.
- Giant Body Slam: cost 9, damage 10.
- Giant Splash: cost 11, damage 15.
- Giant Splash density reduced to 1 copy.
- HP reduced to 60.

### Randy Savage
- Flying Crossbody: cost 6 and draws 2.
- Diving Double Axe Handle: cost 7, gains +1 Attitude on connection, still searches Flying Elbow.
- Flying Elbow Drop: cost 9.
- No HP increase; improvement is entirely aerial flow/card economy.

- Tombstone Piledriver: cost 9.
- Old School density increased to 4 copies.
- No HP increase; improvement is sequence reliability and momentum flow.
