# WWE Legacy v0.12.75 — Booster Collation Hotfix

Pack-collation correction on top of v0.12.74.

- **A standard five-card booster can now contain at most one Very Rare (4★) card.**
- The one-4★ ceiling includes wrestler Finishers, wrestler Specials, Entrances and Superstar chase cards.
- If a Superstar chase card appears, it consumes the pack's Very Rare slot; the remaining ordinary slots are rolled from Common/Uncommon/Rare only.
- If an ordinary Very Rare appears first, later slots cannot produce another Very Rare.
- The existing per-slot rarity weights remain **50% Common / 30% Uncommon / 15% Rare / 5% Very Rare** until the pack's Very Rare ceiling is reached. Afterward the remaining three eligible rarity weights are naturally renormalized.
- The guaranteed first-slot Foil rule, Entrance Foil rule, Superstar 5% chase chance and 50-pack pity remain unchanged. The hotfix removes multi-4★ jackpot packs without reducing the existing chance for the first Very Rare hit.
- This is an economy/collation hotfix only. No card rarity assignments, gameplay values, decks, ownership caps, HP, sets or card IDs change.
