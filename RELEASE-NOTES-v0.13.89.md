# WWE Legacy v0.13.89 — Welcome Full Superstar Start

Released: 21 August 2026

- Expands the one-time Welcome choice to **Evolution, New Generation, Golden Era, Attitude Era, and SummerSlam**.
- Welcome selection remains random within the chosen set.
- Choosing SummerSlam excludes the already-selected CM Punk/Roman Reigns starter, leaving **7 eligible SummerSlam Superstars**.
- The Welcome reward now grants the selected Superstar plus a complete playable **60-page Normal deck**, giving every fresh profile two full playable Superstars.
- All Welcome deck pages are Normal; no Emerald, Sapphire or Ruby cards are pre-granted.
- Fresh deck materialization respects the five-copy-per-tier ownership cap. Only excess authored Momentum duplicates are redistributed where required to keep older blueprints legal at 60 pages.
- No balance, reward-economy, live-set, CPU tier-scaling, Season Road or pack-odds changes.
