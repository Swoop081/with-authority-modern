## v0.13.18 certification focus

- First Superstar continues to receive the complete authored 60-page onboarding deck.
- Every later normal Superstar unlock installs a legal owned 60-page starter without granting the complete recommended chase deck.
- Starter package gives one additional copy of each Superstar-exclusive identity card and uses shared Common/Uncommon replacements for missing recommended copies.
- Complete decks and legitimately owned cards from older builds are never clawed back.
- Legacy identity-only Store unlocks with no playable 60-page deck migrate to the new starter package.
- Deck Lab compares the live deck to the authored recommended blueprint and shows matched slots, missing cards, owned upgrades and active replacements.
- Pulling recommended Finishers, Trademarks, Actions or other blueprint cards can safely replace starter filler through Deck Assistance.
- Pulling the authored Superstar Entrance recommends it over Amazing Entrance; Ask/Auto/Manual assistance modes retain their established behavior.
- Booster Superstar starter packages are deferred until all five pulls are rolled, preventing the unlock grant from altering later cards in the same booster.
- No card balance, HP, economy prices, duplicate values, booster rarity odds, release dates or artwork assets changed.
