## Superstar identity
**Power & Technique** — the first time each Control sequence British Bulldog Connects with a Technical Move, his next Strength Move costs 1 less that Control sequence. The discount is one-use and cannot retrigger again in that same Control sequence.

British Bulldog's authored 60-page deck uses **6 Strength / 4 Technical / 1 Strike / 1 Agility Momentum**, nine dedicated Counter pages and the approved Strength/Technical move mix. His Lead Off 5 is Strength Momentum, Technical Momentum, Hammerlock Takedown, Running Shoulder Block and Side Suplex.
