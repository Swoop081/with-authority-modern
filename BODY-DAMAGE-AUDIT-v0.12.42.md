# v0.12.42 — Persistent Weapon / Environment Damage Audit

Weapon and environment injury is **one-shot persistent body-part damage**. It does not create a maintainable hold and does not automatically submit a wrestler; it simply remains on that body part and can matter if a later Submission targets it.

| Card | Injury | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Belt Whip | Back | +1 | Move / impact |
| Brass Knuckles | Head | +1 | Special / foreign object |
| Hammer in the Boot | Head | +1 | Special / foreign object |
| Loaded Mask Headbutt | Head | +1 | Move / impact |
| Jar of Teeth | Head | +1 | Special / foreign object |
| Throw Into Steel Steps | Back | +1 | Move / impact |
| Throw Into Ringpost | Head | +1 | Move / impact |
| Steel Chair to the Back | Back | +1 | Move / impact |
| Steel Plate | — | — | Weapon setup; the injury is applied by **Loaded Mask Headbutt**. |

### Locked mappings

- **Throw Into Ringpost → Head**
- **Throw Into Steel Steps → Back**
- **Steel Chair to the Back → Back**
- **The Rock — Belt Whip → Back**
- **Loaded Mask Headbutt → Head**
- **Brass Knuckles → Head**
- **Hammer in the Boot → Head**
- **Jar of Teeth → Head**

Existing intentionally body-targeting non-weapon Moves such as Glasgow Kiss, Draping DDT, Punt Kick, Samoan Spike, Shining Wizard, Flair Chop, Elbow to the Back of the Head and Chops in the Corner retain their existing persistent body-part effects.
