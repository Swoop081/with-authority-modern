# WWE Legacy v0.12.74 — Exclusive Rarity Pass

Collector-rarity policy pass on top of v0.12.73.

- **Wrestler-exclusive cards can no longer be Common or Uncommon.** Any card restricted to one Superstar now has a minimum rarity of **Rare (3★)**.
- **Exclusive Trademarks are Rare (3★)**.
- **Finishers remain Very Rare (4★)**.
- **Wrestler Special cards are now Very Rare (4★)**.
- Existing wrestler-exclusive cards already at the correct Rare/Very Rare tier are unchanged.
- Generic/shared cards keep their existing rarity unless separately authored otherwise; the policy does not automatically promote universal utility cards merely because their `kind` is `special`.
- This pass changes collector rarity and booster-pool composition only. Card costs, damage, effects, ownership caps, deck composition, HP, sets and card IDs are unchanged.
