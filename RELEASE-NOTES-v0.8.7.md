# WWE Legacy: Collectible Card Game — v0.8.7

## Superstar unlock celebration
- Unlocking a Superstar now queues a full-screen reward celebration.
- Rewards are shown one card at a time with a Next button: Superstar, Entrance, fixed Lead Off cards, then Finisher/Trademark identity cards.
- Booster Superstar pulls interrupt the normal pack flow to make the unlock a major moment, then return to the pack.

## Foil-only unique cards
- Superstar cards are now Foil-only. There is no Normal Superstar version.
- Entrance cards are now Foil-only. There is no Normal Entrance version.
- Entrance cards have been removed from boosters entirely; they are granted with their Superstar.
- Existing saves migrate owned Superstar/Entrance cards to their single Foil copy.
- Superstar booster pulls are intrinsically Foil.

## Season 1 final reward
- Tier 50 is now reserved for The Rock — The Final Boss.
- The reward is defined as a Season 1 completion-exclusive full-deck Superstar, replacing the previous Tier 50 booster reward.
- The Rock is explicitly excluded from normal booster availability.
- Post-season availability is reserved for the future Store using in-game currency, matching the planned later-season model.
- The entitlement/reward framework is in place; The Rock's actual 55-card gameplay deck/card data still needs to be authored before he can be played.
